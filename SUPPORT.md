# Mujassam — Immersive Geometry

مُجسَّم — مختبر الهندسة

Structured immersive geometry lessons for Grades 5 and 7, Arabic and English interfaces, and Anas Arabic narration.

Explore shapes and solids, cube and cuboid properties, unit cubes, layers and volume reasoning with guided practice and feedback.
