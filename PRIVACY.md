## Data processed

The educational content works without an in-app account. Users select a grade and section and enter a numeric learner code. Use a practice code rather than a name, national identifier or phone number. Local practice logs and summaries contain that code, grade, section, session identifier, event timestamps, interface language, device model, app version, activities, answers and explanations, attempts, hints, assistance requests, activity duration and completion status.

Head and controller tracking supplied by the VR system enables real-time interaction. This version does not record audio or video, or retain continuous head or hand movement paths in its practice logs. Anas narration consists of prerecorded audio files.


## Purpose, storage and sharing

Records support practice progress and activity review. They remain in the app's local data on the headset. This version does not automatically upload them to the developer or a school server. It has no advertising, chat, multiplayer or in-app purchases, and does not read the Meta user profile or friends list. The app does not sell practice records or use them for advertising.

The headset owner or school administrator may access or manually extract files. If a school associates codes with student names, exports records or uses them in research, it is responsible for its own permissions, privacy practices and copies. The app does not automatically perform those transfers or associations.


## Retention and deletion

This version has no automatic retention expiry for local records; they remain until app data is deleted. All users and guardians, in any region, may request help accessing or deleting data free of charge by emailing [zuhdi.zahdeh@gmail.com](mailto:zuhdi.zahdeh@gmail.com?subject=Mujassam%20data%20deletion). Identify the app and your request, and provide only necessary details. Do not send a student's identity document.

For a headset you own, use the device's clear-app-data option where available, or uninstall the app. For school-owned headsets, contact the device administrator. The developer cannot remotely erase files on your headset. Separately remove exported school/user copies and any system backups through the responsible operator. For information you sent to support, the developer will handle your deletion request and explain any legal or technical reason that prevents completion.


## Children and classroom use

The activities support Grades 5 and 7 with appropriate teacher or guardian supervision. The app is not intended for children under 10. Follow Meta and device age requirements and parent-managed account requirements. The app does not ask learners for real names or contact details. A numeric learner code is not age verification; the supervising adult must ensure appropriate use.


## Support and independent services

Support emails include your email address and the content you choose to send, which are used to respond and handle your request. Meta's store and headset operating system, the email provider and this page's hosting provider operate under their own policies and may process technical or account data outside the app's control. Review their privacy and backup settings. This page contains no student-data collection forms or developer-added analytics.
